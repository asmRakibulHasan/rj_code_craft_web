RJ Code Craft ("we", "us", "our") built the Prank Sounds - Prank Unlimited app as a free application. This page tells you what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. If you contact us by email for support, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

Information collected automatically

When you use the app, certain information is collected automatically by us and by the third-party services listed below:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, language, and country
  • App usage events, for example which screens are opened and which features are used
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information, including the state of the app at the time of a crash
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, used only to serve region-appropriate advertising

We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, photos, or microphone audio.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate and display the app's features
  • Show advertisements and measure their performance
  • Understand which features are used most so we can improve the app
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state — required to load content and serve advertisements
  • Notifications — to send you occasional updates about new sounds and features. You can turn these off at any time in your device settings.
  • Storage / Media access — only used if you choose to save a sound to your device or set it as a ringtone or notification tone. Files you save stay on your device and are not uploaded to us.

You can revoke any of these permissions from your device's app settings. Some features may not work if a permission is denied.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics — https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google's advertising partners and how they use data are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit interest-based advertising at any time on your device under Settings → Google → Ads, where you may delete or reset your advertising ID.

Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained by our service providers for the period allowed by their own retention settings, and are deleted or anonymised afterwards. We do not maintain our own user database.

Because the app does not create accounts, there is no stored profile to delete. To remove all locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear its data from your device settings.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
